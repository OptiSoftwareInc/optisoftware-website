Technology Strategy

Enterprise Architecture Strategy

Architecture is useful when it connects business direction to technical decisions and delivery.

OptiSoftware brings cloud, application, database and integration considerations into enterprise architecture. The work establishes decision context, identifies dependencies and aligns modernization priorities with reliability, security, scalability and operational requirements.

Recommendations are shaped by the systems and constraints in the environment rather than a single preferred technology pattern.

Established & Modern Platforms

Cloud & Hybrid Modernization

Modernize Without Starting Over.

OptiSoftware understands modern AWS and application architectures alongside established enterprise environments, including IBM mainframe systems, Db2 for z/OS, Db2 LUW, Java EE, WebLogic, JMS and Oracle.

Mainframe modernization is a cross-platform architecture concern, not a fifth core practice or an automatic migration to AWS. Depending on the workload and business case, a practical path may retain and optimize, integrate, API-enable, rehost or replatform, selectively refactor or migrate, or fully migrate when justified.

Modernization choices

  • Retain and optimize
  • Integrate existing platforms
  • API-enable capabilities
  • Rehost or replatform appropriately
  • Refactor or migrate selectively
  • Replace when justified

Roadmap inputs

  • System and data dependencies
  • Migration sequencing
  • Technical debt
  • Security requirements
  • Service reliability
  • Operations and ownership
