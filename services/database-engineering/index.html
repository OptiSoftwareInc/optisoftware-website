Database Modernization Strategy

Modernize the data platform in step with the applications and business processes that rely on it.

Modernization can mean optimizing a database in place, improving its architecture, integrating it with new services, changing its schema, moving selected data or migrating a workload. The appropriate path depends on workload characteristics, dependencies, availability needs and business priorities.

Database Migration

Database Migration & Cloud Migration

Migration planning begins with data, application dependencies and service continuity—not just a target platform.

OptiSoftware helps assess database and schema dependencies, data movement, migration sequencing and target cloud architecture. Migration may be coordinated with application changes or performed selectively to support hybrid operation and manageable transition risk.

A database that remains fit for purpose may be optimized or integrated in place rather than moved simply because an application is modernizing.

Operational Architecture

High Availability, Reliability & Operations

A database architecture must account for how the system is recovered, secured, monitored and changed.

Availability and operational design should reflect business service requirements, failure modes, data protection needs and the team's ability to operate the platform.

Cloud recommendations connect AWS database and data services to workload architecture rather than treating a database move as an isolated infrastructure task.

Enterprise Data Experience

IBM Db2 Across Distributed & Mainframe Environments

Database modernization benefits from understanding the platforms and dependencies already in place.

OptiSoftware brings IBM Db2 experience across Db2 LUW and Db2 for z/OS, including the application and integration context around distributed and mainframe data. Modernization choices can include retaining and optimizing, API-enabling, integrating, selectively migrating or replatforming based on technical and business requirements.
